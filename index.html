Expected weekly profit

Decision tree

How it works

  1. You choose to order 100, 200 or 300 cases before knowing demand.
  2. Demand then turns out to be 100, 200 or 300 cases. You sell the smaller of what you ordered and what customers want. Each unsold case returns its salvage value (a negative value is a disposal cost). Unmet demand earns nothing.
  3. For each combination:
    profit = price × cases sold + salvage value × cases unsold − cost × cases ordered
  4. For each order quantity, the three profits are weighted by their probabilities and added. That is the expected profit.
  5. The app recommends the order with the highest expected profit.

Expected profit is a long-run average. Any single week can come out higher or lower.
